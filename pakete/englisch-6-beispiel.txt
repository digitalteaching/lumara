# Leuchtturmbucht – junge Fischerin mit Netz und Kopftuch
NAME: Maren
THEMA: Simple present und Häufigkeitsadverbien
HALLO: Ahoy! I'm Maren, the keeper of the bay.
HALLO: Nobody walks into the fog without a test.
HALLO: Show me what you know about the simple present!
SIEG: Well done! You really know your stuff.
NIEDERLAGE: Not yet! Practise a little and come back.
REVANCHE: Back again? This time I'm ready!

FRAGE: She ___ (play) tennis every Monday.
ANTWORT: plays
FALSCH: play | playing | played

FRAGE: My brother ___ (watch) TV after school.
ANTWORT: watches
FALSCH: watch | watchs | watching

FRAGE: We ___ (go) to the beach on Sundays.
ANTWORT: go
FALSCH: goes | going | gos
