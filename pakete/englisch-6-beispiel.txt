# Leuchtturmbucht – junge Fischerin mit Netz und Kopftuch
NAME: Maren
THEMA: Simple present und Häufigkeitsadverbien
HALLO: Ahoy! I'm Maren, the keeper of the bay.
HALLO: Nobody walks into the fog without a test.
HALLO: Show me what you know about the simple present!
SIEG: Well done! You really know your stuff.
NIEDERLAGE: Not yet! Practise a little and come back.
REVANCHE: Back again? This time I'm ready!

FRAGE: She ___ (play) tennis every Monday.
ANTWORT: plays
FALSCH: play | playing | played

FRAGE: My brother ___ (watch) TV after school.
ANTWORT: watches
FALSCH: watch | watchs | watching

FRAGE: We ___ (go) to the beach on Sundays.
ANTWORT: go
FALSCH: goes | going | gos

FRAGE: Tom ___ (not / like) fish.
ANTWORT: doesn't like
AUCH RICHTIG: does not like
FALSCH: don't like | doesn't likes | not likes

FRAGE: ___ your dad work in a shop?
ANTWORT: Does
FALSCH: Do | Is | Are

FRAGE: ___ you have a pet?
ANTWORT: Do
FALSCH: Does | Are | Is

FRAGE: Which word means „immer“?
ANTWORT: always
FALSCH: never | often | sometimes
FORM: auswahl

FRAGE: Which word means „nie“?
ANTWORT: never
FALSCH: always | usually | often
FORM: auswahl

FRAGE: Which word means „manchmal“?
ANTWORT: sometimes
FALSCH: often | never | usually

FRAGE: Wie heißt „normalerweise“ auf Englisch?
ANTWORT: usually

FRAGE: Wie heißt „oft“ auf Englisch?
ANTWORT: often

FRAGE: The cat ___ (sleep) on my bed.
ANTWORT: sleeps

FRAGE: Lisa ___ (fly) her kite on windy days.
ANTWORT: flies
FALSCH: flys | fly | flying

FRAGE: My parents ___ (not / drive) to work.
ANTWORT: don't drive
AUCH RICHTIG: do not drive
FALSCH: doesn't drive | don't drives | not drive

FRAGE: He ___ (do) his homework in the evening.
ANTWORT: does

FRAGE: Bilde den Satz: „Sie ist nie zu spät.“

# Flüsterwald – Waldläufer mit Kapuze und Moosumhang

SIEG: Great! The forest is listening to you now.
